# Lotivity

**Local Activities. At a Price Best for You.**

> Replacing artificial exchanges with real experiences. It's not revolutionary — it's real intelligence.

---

## What It Is

Lotivity connects people to real-world activities, clubs, and events happening in their radius — matched to their generation, heritage, interests, and stage of life. Businesses in the network sponsor the gatherings that align with their values, and users earn discounts by contributing honest voice-memo reviews and profile posts.

## Core Concepts

### Generation Categories
Initial vetting covers generation, age, and general interests. Users select the account type that best reflects their life, with a disclaimer explaining that community promotions are tied to choosing accurately:

- **Youth account** — all hosted events and meetups require a community-appointed host or parent with ID verification confirming approval (liability requirement).
- **General adult account**
- **Retired account**

### Club Categories
Clubs and social gatherings are organized into categories so it's always clear what a group actually does in the area — sports, paid events, volunteer work, and social gatherings each get their own tab.

### Activity Requests
Users put out a radius request and the app organizes around it.

> **Example:** A local social group wants to meet to discuss the Bible. Lotivity notifies people in radius with matching religion and generation selections. Once there's enough interest, the app matches the group with a business in network that shares those values — say, a coffee shop — and issues a promo code (`lotivityclubchrist`) funded by a portion of the app's advertising budget. With enough community upvotes, the group gets sponsored outright.

The goal is demonstrable conversion metrics and an app that visibly invests in its own mission.

### Work Tab
Bridges people across industries and companies for events and activities — from a voted-on work happy hour to company-paid activity bundles.

### Business Programs
Businesses can create and list programs on the app through a marketing subscription — the first paid tier.

### Intentional Marketing
The social side follows people in your active Lotivity social groups, work groups, and event trial posts. If 10,000 people attend an event, you see those event posts for a week — but only if you attended or expressed interest. Reach is earned by relevance, not bid.

### Culture-Based Sponsored Events
Heritage and cultural selections drive real, local sponsorship rather than generic ad targeting.

## Design

### Intro Sequence
Opening the app zooms from an Earth view down into an animated sketch view of a community. The POV flies through:

1. A playground with people gathered
2. A concert venue
3. A coffee shop
4. A pottery class
5. An outdoor running group

…and lands on the profile.

### Profile Creation

**Entry:**
- Link a Google account or Apple ID
- Or float as a guest profile for a broad look at what's going on

**Basics:**
- Name
- Date of birth *(not visible to others)*
- Generation *(visible)*
- Account type as sub-bubbles beside the name: Youth / General Adult / Retired

**Cultural Heritage Association:**
American bubble (or country of location) fills automatically. Heritage bubbles nest under continents:

| Continent | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Europe | Irish, Italian |
| Africa | Nigerian, Tanzanian |
| Asia | Chinese, South Korean |
| Oceania | Australian, New Zealander |
| South America | Brazilian, Colombian |

Heritage selections drive heritage notifications:

> You selected Colombian. On a Friday: *"This Friday, check out Colombian restaurant \_\_\_\_\_\_ for happy hour in your area. 55 people with Colombian heritage voted it a positive experience this past week. Check out the recap highlights →"* — which opens the social feed.

**Language:** Select all languages spoken.

**Religion / Culture bubbles:** Black, Jewish, Christian, Latin, Arab, Muslim, Hindi, and more.

**Relationship status.**

**Interests — pick 6, add more later.** Hobbies, activities, and skills (known or interested in), each with subcategories:

- **Basketball** — organized play, local pickup, outside court, open gym
- **Running** — run club, trails, competitions
- **Reading** — workshops, book clubs, quiet places
- **Music** — genre; concerts, open mic, local bands, lessons
- **Pottery, movies,** and more

**Then:** `Loading… Rendering Your Community…`

## Interface

### For You
Events, recaps, activities, and clubs we think you'll enjoy.

### Groups
- Create a group and invite friends in your network
- Put out a radius request for a group to be organized
- Matching, sponsorship, and promo codes flow as described in [Activity Requests](#activity-requests)

### Social Feed
- **7-day recap**, tied into the radius map — see where your network has been and what they reviewed
- Follow artists to see concert recaps from performances near you, upcoming local shows, and recaps from their shows around the world
- Build a network like LinkedIn from contacts and previous groups
- You can only connect with people in your radius — via invite, automatic contact match, or a previous Lotivity intersection at an event

### Map Radius
Filterable map: **events · clubs · workshops · food**
